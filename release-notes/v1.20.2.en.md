- **Audio presets are back on the Video tab.** A preset saved on the Audio tab (MP3, AAC, FLAC…) disappeared as soon as you loaded a video to extract its sound: the list only showed the presets of the active tab. It now shows every preset usable with the formats that tab offers — so your audio presets are available when extracting the audio of a video, and the other way round. Image presets stay specific to the Image tab, and MP4/MKV presets to the Video tab.
- Detail: editing the metadata of an audio preset from the Video tab now shows the audio fields (album, artist…) instead of the TV series fields.
