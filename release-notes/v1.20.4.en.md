- **The year no longer goes missing from converted files.** Even with "Preserve original metadata" ticked, a song's year could be absent from the converted file's properties, while the title, artist and album showed up normally. The cause: files coming from iTunes or Apple Music store a full date, down to the hour and the second, which Windows cannot make sense of. That date is now formatted properly during conversion.
- **Two further cases fixed along the way.** FLAC files only displayed their year when it was written on its own, with no day or month beside it; and a WMA file's year never appeared in Windows at all, whatever its value, because it was not stored where Windows looks for it. Both are sorted.
- A year typed by hand in the metadata editor still takes precedence: it is never overwritten.
