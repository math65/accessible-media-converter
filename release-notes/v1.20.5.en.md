- **Files with incomplete sound are now flagged as soon as you add them.** Some video files carry a complete picture but an audio track that stops after a few seconds or minutes. Nothing gave it away: their stated duration is correct, they play normally, and the conversion completed without any error before producing an almost silent result. The application now compares the length of every audio track with the video and warns you **before** converting, naming the files concerned.
- **The end-of-conversion check now examines each track separately.** Until now it only looked at the overall length of the produced file, which is the length of its longest track. A conversion whose picture was complete but whose sound had been cut short therefore passed for a success. That gap is closed.
- **The application now keeps a log.** It is saved automatically and attached to your reports, which makes it possible to understand what happened before a problem, not only at the moment it occurred. The contact form benefits too: it previously sent no technical information at all.
- **Three defects fixed in error reports.** The path of the offending file could disappear from the report when the source contained many tracks; the same error message appeared twice; and, most importantly, the automatic diagnostic re-wrote the output file already sitting on your disk. It now works on a temporary copy and no longer touches your files.
